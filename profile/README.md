<h3 align="center">Pearn</h3>
<p align="center">No-loss prediction markets on NEAR. Bet the yield, keep the principal.</p>

Deposit NEAR on YES or NO. The market stakes it with a NEAR validator. When it resolves, everyone gets their principal back and the winning side splits the staking yield, plus any sponsored pot.

Native NEAR staking · time-weighted bets · DAO resolvers · sponsored pots.
