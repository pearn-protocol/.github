<h3 align="center">Pearn</h3>
<p align="center"><b>The no-loss prediction market on NEAR.</b><br>Bet on real questions; win or lose, your stake comes back.</p>

In a normal prediction market the loser loses everything. On Pearn you bet **stNEAR** (Meta Pool's liquid-staked NEAR) on YES or NO. While the market runs, the stake earns NEAR staking yield. When it resolves, **everyone gets their principal's NEAR value back**, and the winning side splits the yield plus anything sponsors added ([what "no-loss" does not cover](https://docs.pearn.site/docs/risks/)).

- **Real questions:** live Polymarket markets are mirrored on Pearn and resolve when Polymarket settles them.
- **NEAR-native yield:** the contracts run on Aurora and read stNEAR's rate from Meta Pool on NEAR through cross-contract calls, with no off-chain price feed.
- **Fair by design:** winnings are weighted by stake × time left, so a late whale can't take early bettors' yield. No owner, no upgrades, no admin access to funds.
